This Again

Some cycles don't break. Jenna and Cal are stuck in a few.

writerSalimah Ismail
directorSalimah Ismail & Tu Nguyen
cinematographerR Jay Molina
stagefestival submissions
synopsis

Jenna and Cal wake up in bed, like they have countless mornings before. When Cal comes to with a jolt and an accusation he can't explain, what starts as an ordinary Sunday argument spirals into something neither of them can walk away from.

This Again is a dark comedy about the violent discomfort of getting a relationship out of a rut.
